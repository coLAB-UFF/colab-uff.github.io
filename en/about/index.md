The Research Laboratory for Communication, Political Cultures and Economies of Collaboration (coLAB/UFF) is a research group created in November 2010 and led, since its founding, by Viktor Chagas, associate professor in the Department of Cultural and Media Studies and in the Graduate Program in Communication at the Fluminense Federal University. coLAB conducts investigations aimed at understanding the interfaces between digital culture and political culture, with an emphasis on studies of contentious repertoires and the strategic uses of affordances on digital platforms, platform culture and online politics, influence operations, and the circulation of disinformation and hate speech, among other topics. Associated with the National Institute of Science and Technology in Digital Democracy (INCT.dd), the group brings together researchers dedicated to the field of political communication, at different levels of training. Its research lines cover studies on: communication strategies and public opinion, political cultures and collective action, and humor and political discussion.

### Institutional Partners

coLAB is responsible for a set of projects developed in an interdisciplinary manner and with the support of its members and inter-institutional partners. Besides the National Institute of Science and Technology in Digital Democracy (INCT.dd), the laboratory is also part of the National Network to Combat Disinformation (RNCD), and maintains close, formal collaboration with the Institute for Technology and Society of Rio (ITS Rio) and FGV Comunicação, through the School of Communication, Media and Information (ECMI); as well as cooperation already carried out with Aláfia Lab, the Image and Data Science Laboratory (Labic) of the Federal University of Espírito Santo; and partner groups and laboratories such as Margem (UFMG), MiDiCom (UFF), GCodes (UFPA), CPOP (UFPR), and more.

More details about some of the activities carried out by coLAB can be found in the [Projects](/en/projects/) section.
