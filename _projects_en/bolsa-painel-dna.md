The DNA Project monitors and analyzes disinformation narratives about vaccination on social media and other digital platforms. Based on the collection and processing of large volumes of data, the initiative develops tools, reports and publicly accessible informational panels, making it possible to track the circulation of this type of content over time. The DNA Project panel systematizes the detection of disinformation narratives about vaccination, making the data and indicators gathered by the team public, in order to support researchers, public managers and civil society in following the phenomenon.
