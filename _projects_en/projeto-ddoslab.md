DDoS Lab is a model agency and innovation and research laboratory focused on combating disinformation and hate speech in the digital environment, with a specific emphasis on identifying and monitoring coordinated political actions, inauthentic behavior and the circulation of attacks on politicians and citizens in general, and anti-democratic messages on social media and private messaging services. Its main goal is to lead digital forensic investigation, social network analysis and big data initiatives in tracking the multiplatform activity of extremist groups.
