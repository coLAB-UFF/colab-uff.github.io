coLAB develops a set of lightweight applications aimed at managing the laboratory itself, all with data stored directly in private GitHub repositories — without depending on external servers or databases. The family currently includes SucupiraLAB (academic management), pqLAB (research routines), xoxoLAB and KrippLAB (collaborative editorial management) and CodLAB (manual coding for content analysis).
