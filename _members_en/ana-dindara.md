Ana Dindara Rocha holds a degree in Archaeology from the Federal University of Aracaju and is currently a master's student in Communication at the Fluminense Federal University (UFF).

A digital activist, she focuses on the analysis of algorithmic racism and hate speech on social media. She began her training at Bamidelê, a pioneering organization of Black women in Paraíba, where she acquired knowledge in strategic communication. She has worked with organizations such as Olabi and Ação da Cidadania.
