Cristhiane Malungo is a PhD student in the Graduate Program in Communication at UFF and holds a master's degree from the Graduate Program in Ethnic-Racial Relations at CEFET-RJ.

She is a civil servant at the Niterói Department of Social Assistance and Human Rights, where she coordinates the Service for Coexistence and Strengthening of Bonds, with teaching experience and work in public administration in Policies for Women — gender, sexuality, ethnic-racial relations, Black feminism and domestic violence. She is currently researching Black feminism and womanism in Brazil.
