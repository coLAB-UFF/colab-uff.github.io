Natalia Dias holds a PhD (2024) and a master's degree (2018) in Communication from the Graduate Program in Communication at the Fluminense Federal University (PPGCOM-UFF), and a bachelor's degree (2013) in Media Studies from the same institution.

She researches communicative practices in the political scene, analyzing the relationship between fan activism and political actions based on collective financing (crowdfunding). She is part of coLAB-UFF and has experience in cultural production, digital marketing, coordination of political campaigns, crisis management and production of national and international events.
