Letícia Sabbatini is a PhD student and holds a master's degree from the Graduate Program in Communication at the Fluminense Federal University (PPGCOM/UFF). She is a professor and researcher at the School of Communication, Media and Information of the Getulio Vargas Foundation (ECMI/FGV).

She is interested in gender studies, political communication and online technologies. She is a member of the editorial board of Revista Contracampo. She is a journalist trained at the Federal Rural University of Rio de Janeiro (UFRRJ), with experience in reporting, photography and digital marketing.
