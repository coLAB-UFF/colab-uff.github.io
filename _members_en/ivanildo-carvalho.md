Ivanildo Carvalho is a PhD student in Communication at UFF, holds a master's and a bachelor's degree in Social Sciences from PUC-Rio, with complementary training in Public Policy Management and Evaluation.

He has experience in the UN-Habitat Social Territories project, in teaching in the state school system (SEEDUC-RJ), and in managing teams and surveys at IBGE. During his master's, he was part of the Center for Studies on Federalism, Politics and Development (NUFEPD/PUC-Rio); in his doctorate, he researches ideological memes and their impact on political disputes, based on moral psychology.
