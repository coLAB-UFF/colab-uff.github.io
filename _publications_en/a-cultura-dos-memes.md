The collection brings together a series of new and original texts, along with republications of articles that helped found the field of meme studies. The book reflects the diversity of approaches and interests that run through the recent development of the field. It is, therefore, a book aimed at those who wish to explore the universe of internet meme research, understand what is important in this debate, and grasp what roles the meme plays in everyday communication.
